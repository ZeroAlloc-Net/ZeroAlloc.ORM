# v2.x Postgres benchmark results

**Status:** Captured 2026-10-15 at 2.3.1, from `main` at `026856b`. This is the first Postgres capture.

> Allocations are exact. Speed is shown as same-run ratios, because the benchmark host is a shared
> VM; absolute timings are not published.

ZA.ORM is a **relative-position** tool: compare the rows of one table, never numbers across tables
or machines.

## Environment

```
BenchmarkDotNet v0.15.8, Linux Debian GNU/Linux 13 (trixie)
Intel Core Ultra 7 255H 3.69GHz, 1 CPU, 12 logical and 12 physical cores
.NET SDK 10.0.402
  [Host]     : .NET 10.0.13 (10.0.13, 10.0.1326.50504), X64 RyuJIT x86-64-v3
```

- The host is a KVM virtual machine on a shared machine, and other workloads, including other Postgres containers, ran during the capture.
- BenchmarkDotNet's default job ran out of process, with three launches per benchmark.
- The benchmark process was pinned to four vCPUs with `taskset -c 2-5`.
- Each launch boots its own Testcontainers `postgres:16-alpine` container through Docker 29.9.0. The container was not pinned, so the server shares the remaining vCPUs with everything else.
- Packages: Npgsql 10.0.3, Testcontainers.PostgreSql 4.16.0, Dapper 2.1.89 and Dapper.AOT 1.1.0.

## Reproduction

```bash
# From repo root; Docker must be reachable.
dotnet build -c Release tests/ZeroAlloc.ORM.Benchmarks
cd tests/ZeroAlloc.ORM.Benchmarks
taskset -c 2-5 dotnet run -c Release --no-build -- --anyCategories Postgres --job Default --launchCount 3
```

## How to read the tables

- **Ratio and RatioSD** are copied from BenchmarkDotNet: each row's mean time divided by
  `HandWrittenAdoNet`'s, averaged over the three launches.
- **Per-launch ratio** is the same ratio computed from each launch's mean separately.
- **Allocated** is the range of the three launches' bytes per operation, from BenchmarkDotNet's GC
  counters. Unlike the Sqlite run, these are not whole numbers that repeat exactly: the counter is
  process-wide, and Npgsql's socket I/O completes on other threads, so each launch picks up a few
  bytes of background allocation. The spread is at most about 1% of the figure.
- **Alloc Ratio** is copied from BenchmarkDotNet.

Every query here is a round trip to a server on the same VM, so the time ratios are much less
stable than on Sqlite: BenchmarkDotNet reports RatioSD around 0.3 for every row, and BenchmarkDotNet
found bimodal or multimodal distributions for most rows. Treat any time ratio within that spread as
parity.

## PostgresSingleRowReadBench

_Workload:_ `SELECT Id, CustomerId, Total FROM Orders WHERE Id = @id` against real Npgsql and `NUMERIC(18,4)`.

| Method            | Ratio | RatioSD | Per-launch ratio | Allocated       | Alloc Ratio |
|------------------ |------:|--------:|-----------------:|----------------:|------------:|
| HandWrittenAdoNet |  1.05 |    0.32 | 1.00             | 1,664 – 1,671 B |        1.00 |
| ZeroAlloc_ORM     |  1.07 |    0.32 | 0.95 – 1.08      | 1,979 – 2,000 B |        1.19 |
| Dapper_AOT        |  1.07 |    0.31 | 0.98 – 1.09      | 1,965 – 1,966 B |        1.18 |

## PostgresMultiRowReadBench

_Workload:_ 1000-row SELECT over a `NUMERIC(18,4)` column into `List<OrderRow>`.

| Method            | Ratio | RatioSD | Per-launch ratio | Allocated         | Alloc Ratio |
|------------------ |------:|--------:|-----------------:|------------------:|------------:|
| HandWrittenAdoNet |  1.04 |    0.28 | 1.00             | 49,451 – 49,579 B |        1.00 |
| Dapper_AOT        |  1.14 |    0.31 | 1.07 – 1.14      | 58,441 – 58,521 B |        1.18 |
| ZeroAlloc_ORM     |  1.23 |    0.36 | 1.12 – 1.29      | 49,935 – 50,128 B |        1.01 |

ZA.ORM allocates within 1% of hand-written ADO.NET, and Dapper.AOT 18% more. ZA.ORM was the slowest
of the three in all three launches.

## PostgresMultiResultSetBench

_Workload:_ head and 10 lines through one `;`-joined batch, Npgsql's multi-result-set path.

| Method            | Ratio | RatioSD | Per-launch ratio | Allocated       | Alloc Ratio |
|------------------ |------:|--------:|-----------------:|----------------:|------------:|
| ZeroAlloc_ORM     |  0.87 |    0.28 | 0.70 – 1.00      | 4,503 – 4,504 B |        1.52 |
| Dapper_AOT        |  1.02 |    0.28 | 0.91 – 1.02      | 3,167 – 3,171 B |        1.07 |
| HandWrittenAdoNet |  1.04 |    0.32 | 1.00             | 2,967 – 2,990 B |        1.00 |

The time ratio for ZA.ORM is the least stable in the suite: 1.00 in the first launch, 0.70 and 0.81
in the other two. ZA.ORM allocates about 1.5 KB more than hand-written ADO.NET here, the largest
allocation gap in either backend.

## Takeaways

- **Time:** on a real network round trip all three are close. Only the multi-row read separates them reliably, with ZA.ORM 12–29% behind hand-written ADO.NET in every launch.
- **Allocation:**
  - ZA.ORM matches hand-written within 1% on the multi-row read.
  - It allocates 6% more on inserts and 19% more on single-row reads.
  - It allocates 52% more on the multi-result-set batch.
